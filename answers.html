Common questions Columbus homeowners ask before they pick a slab bid

Is mudjacking the same as poly lifting?

No. Mudjack uses a heavy slurry through larger holes. Polyurethane foam goes through smaller holes and expands. Ask which one is on the paper.

When should I replace instead of lift?

Shops often say replace when the slab is badly cracked, heaved by roots, or too thin. Ask why this slab cannot be lifted. This site is not engineering advice.

Will a lift last through Ohio freeze-thaw?

Ask what the written warranty covers if the slab drops after winter and whether drainage work is required to keep the paper.

Do I need a City of Columbus permit to lift a driveway?

Ask the shop who pulls any city or township permit if one applies, and whether that line is in the bid. This site does not pull permits.

Should I pick the cheapest first visit?

Public threads compare lift cash against a full tear-out because a cheap lift on a failed slab can still leave a replace later. Ask for both numbers in writing.
